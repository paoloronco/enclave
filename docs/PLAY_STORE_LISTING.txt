FULL DESCRIPTION
─────────────────────────────────
Your passwords belong to you — not to a server, not to a company, not to the cloud.

Enclave is a password manager that works entirely on your device. No account.
No internet connection. No subscription. Just a clean, fast vault that opens
with your fingerprint and keeps your data exactly where it should be: with you.


── WHY ENCLAVE ──────────────────────────────

Most password managers sync your data to remote servers. That means your
passwords travel across networks, sit in databases, and depend on companies
you have to trust.

Enclave takes the opposite approach. Your vault never leaves your phone.
Not even once.


── WHAT YOU GET ─────────────────────────────

  Instant access — unlock with fingerprint, face, or PIN in seconds

  Clean organization — group passwords by category, search instantly

  Safe editing — add titles, usernames, passwords, and private notes

  Auto-lock — vault locks automatically when you step away

  Backup & restore — export an encrypted backup file, restore anytime

  No ads. No tracking. No account required.


── FOR THE SECURITY-MINDED ──────────────────

Every entry in your vault is individually encrypted with AES-256-GCM
before it's saved to disk. Encryption keys are generated and stored
inside Android Keystore — a hardware-backed secure element on your device.
Those keys never leave the chip, not even during backup.

The app requests no internet permission. It cannot phone home.
FLAG_SECURE prevents your vault from appearing in screenshots
or the app switcher.


── YOUR DATA, YOUR RULES ────────────────────

Forget your PIN? There is no recovery — by design. No backdoor,
no "forgot password" email, no master key held by anyone else.
Your vault is yours alone.

Uninstall the app and every byte is gone. No residue on any server.
No one to call. No account to delete.


── ──────────────────────────────────────────

Enclave is free. No premium tier. No upsell. Just a tool that does
one thing well, and stays out of your way.
